About

Twenty years in technology, and still more interested in people.

I spent the early part of my career at Apple, and eventually started Soulcraft, a marketing agency in the Bay Area.

I studied business at Wharton. I also write poetry, which my résumé never knew what to do with.

Work

Soulcraft

Soulcraft helps brands get named and cited, in Google and in the answers people now get from AI.

It rests on one belief: tactics expire and identity compounds. Every engagement begins with who a company is, and I lead each one myself.
